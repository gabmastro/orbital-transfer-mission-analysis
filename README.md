# Orbital Transfer Mission Analysis

Academic group project developed during the BSc in Aerospace Engineering at Politecnico di Milano.

The project focused on the analysis and comparison of different orbital transfer strategies for a satellite from an initial orbit to a prescribed final orbit.

## Project Overview

Several orbital manoeuvre strategies were implemented and compared in MATLAB with the objective of evaluating their performance in terms of:

- Total velocity change (Δv)
- Propellant-related efficiency
- Transfer time
- Overall mission trade-offs

The analysed strategies included combinations of orbital plane changes, changes in orbital shape and orientation, bitangent transfers, secant transfers and bi-elliptic-type manoeuvres.

## Tools and Methods

- MATLAB
- Orbital mechanics
- Numerical methods
- Coordinate and orbital-element transformations
- Iterative numerical solution methods
- Engineering trade-off analysis
- Data analysis and comparison of alternative solutions

Custom MATLAB functions were developed to perform orbital transformations and simulate the different manoeuvre sequences.

Numerical techniques were also used where an analytical solution was not directly available, including an iterative method for determining the intersection conditions required by the secant-transfer strategy.

## Results

Multiple transfer strategies were evaluated by comparing their total Δv requirements and transfer times.

The analysis showed that different solutions offered different advantages: one secant-transfer configuration provided the lowest overall impulse requirement, while another provided a significantly shorter transfer time.

The final strategy was selected by considering the trade-off between mission duration and propellant expenditure rather than optimising a single parameter.
